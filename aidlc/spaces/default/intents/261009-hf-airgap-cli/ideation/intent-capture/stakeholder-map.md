# Stakeholder Map

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest | Authority | Source |
|-------------|----------|-----------|--------|
| Requester, who builds both the download tool and the web app | Reading model details offline and moving model files across the gap reliably | Decides scope and priority alone | [Q2] [Q5] [Q6] |
| Whoever runs the transfer into the air-gapped environment | Receives the written description of the bundle contents | Unknown (open question) [assumption] | [Q5] [Q10] |
| Users of the air-gapped web app, who can be anyone including people outside the organisation | Reading model pages offline | Unknown (open question) [assumption] | [Q1] [Q9] |

## Decision-makers

- The requester decides scope and priority alone [Q6]

## Communication Requirements

- A written description of the bundle contents is wanted [Q7]
- Its readers are the requester, as a reference while building the web app later, and the person who runs the transfer [Q10]

## Assumptions & Open Questions

- How much say whoever runs the transfer has over scope or priority is unknown [assumption]
- How much say the web app users have over scope or priority is unknown [assumption]
